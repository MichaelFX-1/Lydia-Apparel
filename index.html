14 — Questions

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Add fabrics to your bag, open the bag panel, and tap Order via WhatsApp. Your selections are auto-attached to the message.

What are your payment methods?

Bank transfer, card, and international wire. Details are confirmed inside your WhatsApp chat.

Do you sell by the yard or by the roll?

Both. Indicate yardage in WhatsApp, and we'll confirm availability and price.

Can I request a swatch first?

Yes — swatch packs of up to 6 fabrics are dispatched for a small handling fee, refundable on full order.

What is your returns policy?

Unopened, uncut rolls may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Cut yardage is final sale.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — to 30+ countries via DHL Express, fully tracked and insured.
